I study how post-training shapes the intelligence and behavior of foundation models. My current research focuses on multimodal reasoning and agentic AI, using reinforcement learning (RL) and other post-training techniques to improve model and agent capabilities. I am particularly interested in agent memory, harness engineering, recursive self-improvement (RSI), and reasoning.

I'm currently a Research Scientist Intern at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, where I design and train an AI4Weather foundation model for the Genesis Mission. Previously, I was an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon AGI Foundations, where I post-trained a 200B-parameter frontier MLLM across more than 100 compute nodes.
