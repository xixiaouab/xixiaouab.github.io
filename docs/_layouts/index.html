Research Interest

My research focuses on post-training techniques (e.g., reinforcement learning) for LLMs and MLLMs. I am currently working on two directions: (i) alignment and reasoning for LLMs and MLLMs, and (ii) long-horizon LLM agents capable of reliable planning, interaction, and adaptation.
