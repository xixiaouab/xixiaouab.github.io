Computer Science · University of Alabama at Birmingham

I study how post-training shapes the intelligence and behavior of foundation models. My current research focuses on multimodal reasoning and agentic AI, using reinforcement learning (RL) and other post-training techniques to improve model and agent capabilities. I am particularly interested in agent memory, harness engineering, recursive self-improvement (RSI), and reasoning.

I am currently a Research Scientist Intern at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, where I design and train an AI4Weather foundation model for the Genesis Mission. Previously, I was an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon AGI Foundations, where I post-trained a 200B-parameter frontier multimodal large language model (MLLM) for multimodal and agentic reasoning using supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL) across more than 100 compute nodes.

I am actively seeking Research Internship opportunities in Summer 2027. If your team is hiring researchers in MLLM / Agentic post-training-related areas, or if you know of relevant opportunities, please feel free to reach out. Thank you!
